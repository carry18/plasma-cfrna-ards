# Third-party data and reference definitions

- Public whole-blood dataset: GSE66890 and platform GPL6244, obtained from NCBI GEO. Source publication: Kangelaris et al., Am J Physiol Lung Cell Mol Physiol. 2015;308:L1102–L1113. doi:10.1152/ajplung.00380.2014. These are third-party public data, not data generated by the cfRNA study.
- MSigDB 2026.1.Hs gene sets in scripts/assets/msigdb_2026.1_Hs.rds are reference annotations only. MSigDB and the constituent collections retain their original usage/citation terms. https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb/ . This repository does not assign a new license to those definitions.
- Published RR_predicted40 and RR_validated7 signatures: Mitsuyama et al., Respiratory Research. 2026;27:277. doi:10.1186/s12931-026-03708-0; Supplementary Table ST6.
- Lung cell-reference sets originate from Travaglini et al., Nature. 2020;587:619–625. doi:10.1038/s41586-020-2922-4, accessed through MSigDB.
- Software dependencies retain their own licenses.
